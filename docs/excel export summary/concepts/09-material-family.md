# 09 · Material Family (and Tiers)

[← Index](README.md) · [Field model](00-field-model.md) · [Spec](../ttrpg-app-spec.md)

> **Purpose:** the crafting substrate. A family (copper, iron, adamantine, fur, leather…) exists at N quality tiers, each carrying [stat](01-stat.md) modifiers and a gold value.

**Tiers are child records** — a tier cannot exist without its family, so it lives on this page rather than its own.

---

## Why it's data, not code

~290 material rows in the sheet are almost all generated by a visible arithmetic pattern, filled down by hand. The patterns are not recorded anywhere, so the only way to add an 11th tier is to reverse-engineer the intent from the numbers — which is exactly what had to be done to write this page.

Recording the generator alongside the values means the pattern is a first-class, editable thing, and the exceptions become visible.

## Material Family fields

| Field | Type | Source | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| `name` | text | literal | ✅ adamantine, copper, electrum, gold, iron, platinum, silver, fur, leather, feather, meat, tanden (teeth), claw… |
| `category` | ref | link | ✅ Metals, Hides, Organics |
| `tier_count` | number | literal | ✅ Seed 10 |
| `usable_for` | link/selector | link | Which [item templates](11-item-template.md) accept it |
| `base_*` | number | literal | Inputs to the tier generators |
| `tier_generators` | map field → formula | formula | Context: `family`, `tier`, `const` |
| `description`, `colour` | text | literal | For display |

## Tier fields

One row per tier, every [stat](01-stat.md) as a column plus `value`.

| Field | Type | Source |
|---|---|---|
| `tier` | number | inherited (1…N) |
| `mods` | map stat → number | generated **or** literal, per-cell override |
| `value` | number | generated or literal |
| `display_name` | text | formula — ✅ `"{family} {tier}"` → `iron 1` |

## Confirmed generators ✅

**Adamantine** — `str = 10 + tier`, `con = 10 + tier`, `dex = −11 + tier`, `char = 10 + tier`, `value = 9000 + 1000 × tier`

| tier | 1 | 2 | 5 | 9 | 10 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Str | 11 | 12 | 15 | 19 | 20 ✅ |
| Dex | −10 | −9 | −6 | −2 | −1 ✅ |
| value | 10000 | 11000 | 14000 | 18000 | 19000 ✅ |
| Health | 6 | 6 | 7 | 8 | 8 ⚠️ |

**Copper** — `mana = 5 + 5 × tier` ✅ (10, 15, 20, 25, 30, 35, 40, 45, 50, 55)

**Electrum** — `char = tier`, `mana = 95 + 5 × tier` ✅, `value = 100 × tier`

## The four anomalies ⚠️

| Family | Field | Pattern | Breaks at | Actual |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| adamantine | Health | no clean formula | throughout | 6, 6, 6, 7, 7, 7, 7, 7, 8, 8 |
| copper | Con | `ceil(tier / 2)` | tier 10 | **6** (pattern says 5) |
| electrum | value | `100 × tier` | tier 10 | **990** (pattern says 1000) |
| — | point-buy sub-type | see [Curve](06-curve.md) | 9 points | 4.642857142857 |

Three of these are in the tier tables alone. Whether they are deliberate balance (adamantine's Health curve looks intentionally flattened) or typos (electrum's 990 looks like a typo) is **your call** — but the design consequence is not optional:

> **Any system that regenerates tiers from a formula without preserving flagged cells silently erases all four.**

Hence the rule: generate, overlay overrides, show both in the editor, and highlight every cell that differs from its generator. Regeneration reports what it kept.

## Seed content ✅

~290 tier rows. Metals run copper → silver → electrum → gold → platinum → iron → adamantine in roughly ascending value (copper tier 1 = 1 gold; adamantine tier 10 = 19,000). Organic families (fur, leather, feather, meat, tanden, claw) come from [harvesting](15-harvest-table.md).

Stat focus differs by family — copper grants Mana, adamantine grants Str/Con/Char at a heavy Dex penalty, electrum grants Char and Mana. That specialisation is what makes material choice a real decision.

## Validation

- A family whose generators produce values outside a sane range flags at save time (preview shows all N tiers before you commit).
- Overridden cells are always visibly marked; a "show only overrides" filter lists every deviation in the system.
- Deleting a family is blocked while any item instance or harvest table references its tiers.

## Editing scenarios

| You want to | You do |
|---|---|
| Extend all families 10 → 12 tiers | Change `tier_count`. Generated families extend; hand-authored ones are flagged for review. |
| Add "mithril" | One record, set base values, generators do the rest. |
| Rebalance all metals | Edit the shared generator on the category. |
| Fix electrum tier 10 | Clear the override; the generator refills 1000. |
| Make tier 11+ superlinear | Change the generator to `base × tier^1.2`; existing overrides survive. |
